# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We welcome respectful, constructive participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Discussions should focus on evidence, reproducibility and the work, not personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient with contributors.
- Give specific, actionable technical feedback.
- Respect privacy and avoid posting credentials, sensitive network observations or private data.
- Credit others' work and follow applicable licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, or sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer through an appropriate private GitHub channel if one is available; do not publish sensitive reports in issues. Maintainers may moderate discussions, remove inappropriate contributions or restrict participation. No guaranteed confidential reporting address or response SLA is claimed here.

This policy applies to project-managed issues, pull requests and community discussions.
